About

Summary

I am a software developer from Bulgaria. I live in Vratsa and build internal software for a sheet-metal fabrication company. In my younger years I competed a lot in IT, Mathematics, and Physics. After school I studied and worked in the Netherlands for a few years, then spent a few more in my family's village helping my grandparents. In my free time I maintain a small ecosystem of self-made tools, and lately I have been learning electronics and 3D printing. My goal is to be able to build complete custom solutions, be they software, hardware, or the enclosures they live in.


Education

During my middle and high school years in Bulgaria (2010-2018), my main focus was on IT, Mathematics, and Physics. I graduated with an average of 5.73 and had the lowest grade in music out of my peers (I still like to believe my musical skill was just hard to conventionally measure).

During my high school years, I went to a lot of extracurricular activities which allowed me to see what I was capable of compared to my peers from all over the country during many competitions. All the learning and competing gave me a lot of amazing memories as well as the opportunity to travel across the country and even abroad it, plus a free admission to our country's most prestigious university when I was graduating.

Despite the aforementioned free admission, I wanted to try my luck abroad so I studied for an English Certificate and earned a C2 Cambridge level.

I ended up going to the University of Applied Sciences - Saxion in Deventer, Netherlands, with a Software Engineering Bachelor (2019-2021). I aced my first year and got the "Getuigschrift propedeuse HBO First Year Certificate". I ended up stopping my studies after the end of my 3rd year's 1st semester around the whole COVID pandemic.

After leaving my studies in the Netherlands I continued to work the part-time job I had but now full-time and dedicated my free time to software projects. With a team from my country, I finished a few projects that are still in active use today.

I returned to Bulgaria in 2022 to help my grandparents in their daily lives. During those years I took my education into my own hands, delving primarily into Python, Node & React. Since 2025 I have continued learning on the job, and lately at my own hardware bench.

  • Part-time Farmer and Caretaker 2022-2025

    The reason for my return to Bulgaria was to help my grandparents, with whom I lived in our village until moving to Vratsa in 2025. The help was hands-on: farming both in our yard and out in the field with a tractor, building work, and animal care (mostly chickens by that point). The work was to scout the enclosure's perimeter for holes that could let predators in, clear debris, feed the deer, and move supplies into the in-enclosure storage. None of this work was paid in money, but I still consider it something worth putting here.

  • Lesto Product, Software Developer 2025-Present

Software I have built there is in daily use across the company: quality control, vending machine management, floor planning, legal-change monitoring, automated database backups, an instruction center, and the company hub. I also help assemble and test the Mini-PCs that go into the vending machines we produce, and I occasionally stray into design (an expo toolkit that became our new business cards and brochures).
